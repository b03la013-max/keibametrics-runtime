## Data actually recovered

Library originals were downloaded read-only and hashed. The research archive
contains 13,710 JRA historical runs, including 13,428 flat runs, of 1,335
parent-composite horse identities with runs. These are sampled career histories
from 2019-06-09 through 2026-10-10, not a complete national five-year population.
Its 113 primary-source references (28 harvests + 85 signed SOURCE files) match
current repository bytes 113/113. Re-importing them would duplicate existing
observations. Existing authenticated corpus ingestion is reused instead.
Current main has 63 harvest files; file count is not authenticated horse coverage.

The workbook, report and research archive are the same research lineage, not
three independent populations. Workbook rows include title/header/footer rows;
16,484 reported blood groups remain insufficient under the research gates.
Parent composite hashes are not official horse IDs. National coverage denominator,
lineage/inbreeding and historical odds/payout coverage remain unproven.

The recovered Blood-B archive contains only 2026-10-10/11 Tokyo/Kyoto: 48 races,
659 listed rows, 573 payout rows. It is post-result descriptive data. Its permission
statement does not establish public redistribution or JRA Production admission.
No private provider records were copied into GitHub or granted formal authority.
A separate complete five-year Blood-B raw corpus was not located in the discovered
Library files; it must not be invented from these exports.

LOCAL PR253 is merged. Its 2021–25 reference is 750 annual top-50 rows plus 726
partial overlapping venue supplements, not complete runner-level history. Its
unvalidated AEI and neutral-52 rules are not imported into JRA.

## Correctness repairs

* Later trusted maternal-parent enrichment now fills a missing damsire; conflicting
  known damsires are permanently quarantined for maternal pooling. Sire/dam-based
  identity is retained and source references are preserved.
* Contradictory target card/history parents or horse names stop feature generation
  as identity failures, never as officially unpublished data eligible for DCR.
* Every numerical preparation and future JRA FINAL constructs a BVI consumption
  proof by replaying the unchanged production evaluator. Contributions reconstruct
  canonical BVI exactly, including DCR shrinkage. The signed FINAL additionally
  binds actual KRS receipt, MEC, Capital and ticket transport hashes.
* The legacy evaluator still applies its existing absence/DCR authority. The new
  proof explicitly says an absent feature's cause is not independently established;
  it does not falsely certify all absences as genuinely unpublished. Acquisition
  failure/evaluator rejection cannot be certified as observed pedigree use.
* A supplied but identical B-arm input no longer counts as an enhanced distinct
  input. Evidence, BVI and ranking differences are measured separately.
* Actual compact RESULT envelopes sign the result-request hash and do not embed an
  artifact or family field. Forward settlement now verifies that exact binding and
  calls the real runtime verifier. Every read rechecks authenticity; corrupt races
  receive individual HOLD records while other races continue.
* Old prospective policy cohorts are preserved separately from current policy.
  No historical freeze, signed SOURCE, signed FINAL or RESULT request is rewritten.

## Measurements and interpretation

23 archived formal requests / 313 runners replay successfully: 277 measured,
14 measured+DCR, 22 DCR-only. ESTABLISHED:209/11/14;
LOW_CAREER:67/3/8; NEWCOMER:1/0/0. These are archived-input replays,
not a new OIDC verification of all 23 SOURCE files or proof of predictive utility.
Per-runner classifications, canonical BVI hashes and reconstruction sums are in
historical_consumption.json. Detailed rules/sample counts remain in unchanged
canonical requests and are exposed by build_bvi_consumption. Missing counts stay UNKNOWN.

BVI directly enters TPI (0.05 before existing adjustments), then SRI, F3S and T3I.
ZAI_WIN / ZAI_PLACE have no direct BVI term. Current Static winner ranking uses
ZAI_WIN. A BVI-only change therefore cannot be assumed to improve Winner ranking.
Other base indices are not automatically improved by extra pedigree data.

Seven genuinely pre-cutoff committed, frozen A/B/C cases from the old policy
fingerprint have actual signed RESULT verification and virtual settlement.
A and B both invested 9,400 yen and returned 360 yen (PFS 3.829787234%).
C has the same financial outcome in this small cohort. No improvement is shown.
Current-policy eligible settled count remains zero; old cases are not relabelled
as evidence for this repair. No purchase is inferred. The existing minimum-30
review gate is not a promise of statistical significance or automatic promotion.

## Acceptance

Focused regressions cover real compact receipt schema, changed-request rejection,
corrupt-race isolation, canonical contribution replay, DCR-only separation,
maternal enrichment/conflict and unchanged current-policy roles. The existing
production-intake CI rechecks real GitHub OIDC and external KRS (5,000 per arm),
then MEC/Capital, using historical diagnostic inputs explicitly outside OOS.
Future official source availability, full five-year completeness, human delivery,
forward predictive improvement and long-run capital improvement remain unproven.
