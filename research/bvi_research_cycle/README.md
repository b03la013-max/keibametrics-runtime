# Continuous BVI research audit — non-Production

The existing postresult schedule can call
`python scripts/jra_bvi_research_cycle.py --settlement-manifest runtime_out/jra_research_settlement_manifest.json`.
It does not create a new schedule, fetch a result, refit a model, call KRS,
create a prediction freeze, add an OOS race or authorize Production. The
existing read-only ChatGPT monitor stays a monitor.

The audit keeps Source-derived, Static Owner and fixed-D ledgers separate,
checks the fixed model raw SHA and all five bound source hashes, registers
seven falsifiable A–G research protocols, and retains the earlier rejected
hierarchical Candidate as a **historical proxy diagnostic**. Its MSE is never
reported as a new evaluation on the current corpus. Parent names in hypothesis
domains come from the immutable fixed publication and remain composite-name
proxies, not permanent official IDs or independent offspring certification.

By default the audit selects the newest valid existing `day-audit-*.json`
Collector summary at or before its audit clock, without another acquisition.
Corrupt or future summaries are quarantined in the output. An optional
`--corpus-summary` accepts the inventory's original self-hashed summary.
The audit rechecks that hash, distinguishes dedicated
harvest observations from all-Reader counts, and explicitly does not repeat
OIDC admission. Collection and support totals cannot make training qualified.
Absent quality evidence is UNKNOWN; hypotheses stay `HOLD_DATA_QUALITY`.
Unknown independent labels and uncertainty are not zero-filled. Each protocol
predeclares controls, primary normalized-finish loss, downstream/economic
metrics, chronological/rolling evaluation, group dependence, rejection and
multiple-testing requirements. No newly registered hypothesis is counted as
tested or supported.

When one fixed-model/current-policy canonical cohort first reaches at least
30, the audit checks its unique race membership against existing immutable
canonical settlement records. It saves one immutable review packet keyed by
the cohort fingerprint and threshold. If periodic execution first observes
more than 30, that actual membership count is preserved; it is not relabelled
as exactly 30. This is a review packet of existing canonical results, **not new
cryptographic OOS admission**. Signature verification is not claimed anew.

The next state is `WAITING_ELIGIBLE_CANDIDATE`. Independent review, a qualified
new dataset, separately frozen model/code/protocol hashes, an independent
prospective publication witness and an approved research path are required.
No model is invented or automatically moved to Forward OOS. Data audits,
hypothesis exploration and the existing collection continue after 30; they do
not depend on a ready successor. A valid successor execution path remains an
external qualification/approval condition.

`current.json` distinguishes `audit_execution_status=VERIFIED_COMPLETE` from
overall research `PARTIAL`. Upstream settlement failures remain visible while
quality audits continue. Operational audit failure saves `BLOCKED`, exits
nonzero and uses a stable incident ID. Repeated identical evidence reuses one
immutable audit and original hypothesis registration timestamps. A corrupt
fixed model, code binding, hypothesis protocol or canonical membership is
rejected. The derived `last_checked_at` still updates on non-race days with
unchanged input, without making a new hypothesis/test/cohort event. Historical
bad performance is retained; rolling fits and new
Candidate fitting are explicitly `NOT_EXECUTED` until qualified data exist.
Self-consistent rehashing is not authority: complete immutable hypothesis,
milestone and audit payloads are compared to recomputed expected content.
Record hashes are bound into the evidence fingerprint; an edited ACCEPTED,
promotion, utility or OOS claim cannot be smuggled through a recomputed hash.
